Hi! I'm a final-year Mathematics and Computer Science undergraduate at Bowdoin College. I attended the Budapest Semesters in Mathematics and finished with honors. I study the geometry of generalization and representation in machine learning, with specific interests in length extrapolation for neural algorithmic reasoning and gradient-based interpolation for implicit neura representations. I use tools from tropical, polyhedral, and optimization geometry.

I am currently a visiting researcher at the Naval Postgraduate School in Monterey, California. Previously I worked on Sign2vec, an expressive embedding space for American Sign Language using spatio-temporal graph convolutional networks, and on the role of age and phonology in memory search using computational models of semantic retrieval.
